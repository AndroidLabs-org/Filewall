Privacy Policy for Filewall: Secure Storage Provider

Effective Date: 12 October 2026

Filewall: Secure Storage Provider is an open-source Android application developed by AndroidLabs. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, store, or analyze any personal data or usage statistics. All operations and user data remain strictly on your device.

2. Permissions Usage

Filewall: Secure Storage Provider uses Android’s Storage Access Framework (SAF) to provide file management and access control capabilities. The app declares these permissions of its own (the Android libraries it uses add two more, USE_FINGERPRINT and a signature-level receiver permission, both described in the Permission Disclosure):

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – to notify you of access requests and blocked access. Optional: if you decline it, the app works without notifications.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC – to let you unlock the app with a fingerprint or face, in addition to the Filewall PIN. Optional.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – to end any open authentication session when the device restarts.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES – to list your installed apps when you create a rule, and to show the name and icon of the app that is asking for access. The list is not stored or sent anywhere.

Filewall does not declare the Internet permission.

3. Authentication

The application allows users to protect access to the app using a PIN code or device-level biometric authentication. This information is never transmitted or stored outside the device and is handled securely via the Android Keystore system.

4. Access Logs

Filewall: Secure Storage Provider may locally log file access events to provide transparency and user control. These logs remain on-device and are not sent anywhere and are not accessible to any third party or developer. They leave your device only if you choose to share them yourself.

5. No Internet Access

The application does not request or require Internet access. It is designed to operate entirely offline and does not connect to external servers.

6. Children’s Privacy

Filewall: Secure Storage Provider is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or process any data related to minors.

7. Open Source

Filewall: Secure Storage Provider is open-source and publicly available on GitHub. Users are encouraged to inspect and audit the code to verify privacy practices.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: info@androidlabs.org
